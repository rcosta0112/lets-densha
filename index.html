About

ご乗車ありがとうございます。

We lived in Tokyo for almost ten years. That was more than ten years ago, before our son was born. It doesn't feel very real now. This train is based on different bits from different things I remember. The ride to Kamakura by the ocean. People looking quietly at their phones. Some of the illustrations on the ads I made on an iPad, riding the train to Kichijōji in the morning.

Made using Threejs and Blender.

Music by Sinneschlösen.

Made by Ricardo Costa in 2023
